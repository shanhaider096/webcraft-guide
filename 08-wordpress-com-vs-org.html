WordPress.com vs WordPress.org: What's the Difference?

Introduction

WordPress.com and WordPress.org are related but represent different ways of using WordPress. Knowing the distinction helps you choose an approach that fits your project.

WordPress.com

WordPress.com provides a hosted service. Hosting and many technical tasks are handled by the service, while customization and features depend on the plan and setup.

WordPress.org

WordPress.org distributes the WordPress software. With self-hosted WordPress, the site owner normally chooses hosting and has more direct control over files, themes, plugins, and configuration.

Which Is Better?

There is no universal answer. A managed service may suit someone who wants fewer technical responsibilities, while self-hosting may suit someone who wants broader control.

What to Compare

Consider total cost, maintenance, customization, backups, performance needs, and any specific plugins or themes you expect to use.

Conclusion

Choose based on your goals rather than the name alone. Understanding the trade-offs before starting can prevent unnecessary migration work later.
